# SEO / AI-search direction

This version is designed around Google's current guidance:
- clear, people-first answers rather than keyword stuffing
- descriptive page titles and headings
- crawlable HTML
- canonical URL
- Open Graph metadata
- LocalBusiness structured data matching visible information
- descriptive service language and location signals
- concise FAQ-style answers that are easy for search systems and AI assistants to understand
- fast, lightweight static architecture
- original photography and first-hand business information

Important: no site can guarantee inclusion or ranking in Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT or other assistants. The goal is to make the business, services, location, expertise and answers exceptionally clear to both people and machines.
