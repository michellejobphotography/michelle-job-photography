# Benchmark findings and changes

I benchmarked the proposed site against 2026 photographer website roundups and examples. Recurring strengths were immersive but restrained imagery, simple navigation, complete/whole-story galleries, transparent starting prices, strong About/process content, trust proof, local/venue relevance, clear enquiry paths, and mobile-first UX.

## Added in V2
- Trust strip immediately below the hero.
- Story-led portfolio cards rather than only a highlights grid.
- Dedicated Weddings, Families and Portraits landing pages.
- Dedicated Stockholm City Hall wedding SEO landing page.
- Four-step client experience.
- Clear investment cards using Michelle's real starting prices.
- Stockholm/location discovery links for local SEO.
- Mobile sticky Email / Enquire / WhatsApp actions.
- Expanded sitemap and service-specific metadata.
- More descriptive image alt text.
- Stronger service + location semantics for Google and AI assistants.

## Intentionally not invented
Testimonials, awards/press logos, star ratings, fake statistics, and AI-generated SEO blog posts. These should only be added when Michelle has real source material.
